Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Flowa bottles.

Product & Materials

What materials are your bottles made from?

Our bottles come in stainless steel, glass, and BPA-free plastic, depending on the collection. Each product page lists the exact material used.

Are Flowa bottles BPA-free?

Yes, all of our plastic bottles are 100% BPA-free and made with food-safe materials.

Is it safe to put hot drinks in a Flowa bottle?

Our insulated stainless steel bottles are designed to safely hold both hot and cold beverages. Glass and plastic bottles are best suited for cold or room-temperature drinks — check the individual product description for specifics.

Care & Maintenance

How do I clean my bottle?

Most Flowa bottles are hand-wash recommended to preserve their finish and insulation. Some stainless steel models are top-rack dishwasher safe — check your product's care instructions.

Can I put my bottle in the freezer?

We don't recommend it. Rapid temperature changes can affect insulation performance and, for glass bottles, may cause cracking.

Orders & Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Standard orders typically arrive within 5–7 business days. You'll receive a tracking link once your order ships.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within our home region — international shipping is something we're working on expanding.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to accommodate changes.

Custom & Bulk Orders

Can I get a custom design on my bottle?

Yes — check out our Custom/Personalized Bottles page for options like engraving, colors, and logo printing.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?

Yes, we offer discounted rates for bulk orders (corporate gifts, events, teams). Visit our Bulk/Wholesale page or contact us directly for a quote.

Returns & Warranty

What's your return policy?

Unused items in original condition can be returned within 30 days of delivery. See our full return policy for details.

Do your bottles come with a warranty?

Most stainless steel bottles include a 1-year manufacturer's warranty against defects. Glass and plastic bottles are covered under standard return policy only.
