About Flowa

Our Story

Flowa started with a simple idea: hydration shouldn't be an afterthought. We design bottles that people actually want to carry - built to last, made responsibly, and styled for everyday life.

Our Mission

At Flowa, we believe staying hydrated should be simple, sustainable, and stylish. Our mission is to create bottles that combine thoughtful design with genuine durability — reducing single-use plastic waste one bottle at a time. Since 2020, we've been committed to building products that fit seamlessly into your daily routine, wherever life takes you.

Our Values

Sustainability

Sustainability — Every bottle is designed with the planet in mind, from materials to packaging.

Quality

Quality — Built to last, tested for everyday durability and real-life use.

Simplicity

Simplicity — Clean, functional design without unnecessary complexity.

Community

Community — We're building more than bottles — we're building a movement toward mindful hydration.
